# Part A — Review this

## 1–3. Summary
**Why:** Store owners already edit their product photos on their phone before uploading, so the in-app editing tools get in the way. Today the upload screen makes them crop every photo and then shows brightness/contrast sliders, a filter dropdown and a "Remove Background" option, and it splits uploads into four fixed boxes (Front, Side, Back, Detail), each uploaded separately. The client wants the photo step to be faster and simpler: no editing, and one place to drop several photos at once.

**Most important result:** An owner can add several product photos in a single action with no editing step, and the listing is still generated by the AI exactly as before.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What changes** | The photo step loses all editing (crop, brightness, contrast, filter, remove-background). The four fixed view boxes become one frame with a large "+". Owners pick several photos at once; the photos appear one after another and can be removed individually before submitting. |
| **We will** | Remove the editing/crop screen · remove the adjustment panel · add one multi-select "+" frame · show photos in the order added · allow removing a single photo · keep the max at 4 · keep large photos uploadable with an automatic, invisible resize. |
| **We won't (this time)** | Add reordering of photos · allow more than 4 photos · change the AI analysis, product submit or Shopify sync · change any other dashboard screen. |
| **Must never happen** | A photo an owner picked is silently lost, or the product is created with the wrong photos. |
| **Must not change** | The AI Vision analysis logic · the product submit flow · the Shopify sync · the leStores Apple app · le-stores.com. |

## 11. What exists today (as a user sees it)
- In **Add Product → Product Details**, owners see a title "Add product image", a note "(User can upload up to 4 images for each product)", an "Upload Multiple Photos" button, and four fixed boxes labelled **Front View, Side View, Back View, Detail View**, each with a camera button and an "Upload" button.
- Picking a photo opens a **crop screen** with a "Crop and Upload" button. After uploading, each photo shows a **brightness slider, a contrast slider, a filter dropdown and a "Remove Background"** checkbox, plus a remove (X) button.
- Selecting several photos at once fills the empty boxes in a fixed Front → Side → Back → Detail order, walking through the crop screen one photo at a time.
- The **same photo screen is used by the guest "Try" demo**.
- On **Next**, the photos feed the AI analysis step, which fills the listing; on Submit the product is created and synced.
- **Finding (requirement 4):** the AI analysis uses an ordered list of up to 4 photo links (first = main photo); it does **not** depend on the view labels or on the edited/cropped versions.

**Things that could break:** the AI analysis step, product creation and Shopify sync, the guest demo flow, and the "only up to 4 / reject videos / stay under the size limit" safeguards — all of these depend on how photos are gathered and held in the shared add-product state.
